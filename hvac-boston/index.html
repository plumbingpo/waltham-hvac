Keeping Your Boston HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
