Upfront Pricing

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Waltham HVAC provides honest, upfront pricing across Waltham, covering everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

The trouble with HVAC failures is that most of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down a little at a time, and a weak capacitor lasts right up until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

Not every call is an emergency. Many Waltham homeowners call us to evaluate an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, replacing on your own schedule beats scrambling for a new unit during a cold snap. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your equipment has left.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Preventing a breakdown beats paying for one. That's the idea behind our maintenance plans. Twice a year, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and change the filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a regular maintenance schedule reduces operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Proper Sizing

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Waltham HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation that accounts for square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

Picking a new system is about more than a SEER2 number. Our technician will compare furnace-and-AC setups with heat pumps, and ducted systems with ductless mini-splits, walking through operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Growing families often do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds tighter temperatures from room to room and uses less energy. The decision is yours, based on your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

The HVAC Repairs Waltham Homes Need Most

The majority of repair calls we take in Waltham come down to a handful of familiar problems. The AC runs but the air never gets cold. The furnace won't ignite or keeps short-cycling. Ice builds up on the evaporator coil, the outdoor unit hums but never kicks on, or a mini-split starts flashing error codes. Add refrigerant leaks, clogged drains, and forgetful thermostats, and you have the full list. We fix every one of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A weak capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
